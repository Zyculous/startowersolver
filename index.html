Algorithms for common cases

Three levels, one method

The package lists three goals. Here is how to reach each one, in order. The full solve uses only about a dozen memorized moves.

The golden rule. The middle ring only changes when you turn it (E) or flip an odd number of times. So once the middle is right, use only U, D and an even number of flips, and it stays right.
Beginner goal

Two matching columns

Turn the top and bottom layers until two columns line up with the middle. If a middle color is missing from a column, a single flip / brings a top or bottom piece into a new column. Experiment freely: two flips in a row always undo each other.

Step 1 · Advanced

Put the middle ring in order

Going clockwise when seen from above, the middle must read white, red, orange, magenta, blue. Turn the middle with E so the two pieces you want to swap sit on either side of the front tip, then flip /. That swaps them. Repeat until the order is right. Top and bottom get mixed up while you do this, which is fine.

Step 2 · Intermediate goal

All yellow on top, all green on the bottom

Each flip trades the front star point and the two front wedges between top and bottom. Use pairs of flips with U and D turns in between, such as / U2 /, to move yellow pieces up. For the last stubborn pieces, use the Point swap and Wedge face swap algorithms. They keep the middle in place but shuffle side colors, which you fix next.

Step 3 · Advanced goal

Line up every column

  1. Turn U to match as many top pieces to the middle as possible.
  2. Fix the rest three at a time with the Top 3-cycle U / U2 / U / U2 /. Turn U before and undo the turn after to aim it at different pieces.
  3. Do the same on the bottom with the Bottom 3-cycle D / D2 / D / D2 /.
  4. If exactly two top pieces and two bottom pieces are swapped, use the Double swap. Swaps always come in such pairs because every flip moves wedges in pairs.

How to read the moves

Hold the puzzle with yellow on top and a top star tip pointing straight at you. The two columns on either side of that tip are the front cap, the chunk cut off by the colored line in the 3D view.

U
Turn the top layer one column (72°) clockwise, looking down from above. u
U'
Top layer one column counter-clockwise. y
U2
Top layer two columns (144°) clockwise. U2' goes the other way.
D
Bottom layer one column clockwise, as if you were looking up at the bottom. d / s for D'.
E
Middle layer, same direction as D. e / w for E'.
/
Flip the front cap 180° top-over-bottom, turning it around the axis that points at you. The cap's top and bottom pieces trade places and its two columns swap left and right. space
Five-sided layers have two turn sizes in each direction. A turn of three columns is the same as U2', and five columns returns to the start.

Position names used in the algorithms

  • Front-left / front-right: the two columns of the front cap.
  • Back-left / back-right: the next column round on each side.
  • Back: the column facing away from you.
